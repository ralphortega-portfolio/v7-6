Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to the details you send through this website.

What I collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, best time to call, type of business, and your message.

Why

To reply to your request and arrange a call with you. I don’t sell your details.

How it is sent

The form is delivered to my email inbox through Web3Forms, a form service that processes your submission on my behalf. Their own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Fonts and cookies

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you visit. The site does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

Your choices

To ask what I hold about you or to have it deleted, message me on LinkedIn.
